Tanout Shop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Tanout Shop ("the app," "we," "us") is a local marketplace for Imintanout, Morocco. Anyone can browse items and food places; sellers post items for sale; food places publish their menu; buyers choose a delivery person (or pick the order up themselves) and send the order on WhatsApp. The app never handles payments. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Who Is Responsible for Your Data

Tanout Shop is developed and operated by Rachid, an independent developer based in Morocco, who is the data controller for the personal data processed by the app. Contact: rachid.aitbaih95@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Browsing without an account. You can browse items, food places and sellers without an account. In that case we do not store any personal information about you. Your language choice is saved only on your phone.

Account (Google Sign-In). An account is needed to order, sell, sell food, deliver, rate or report. When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication receives your name, email address and Google profile photo. Your email address is never shown to other users. Each Google account has one role, chosen when you first sign in: customer or seller, or delivery person.

Public profile. Your profile name, profile photo and the approximate address you type (for example "near the souk") are visible to everyone who uses the app. The app never asks for or shows an exact address or map location.

Phone number (optional). If you add a phone number to your profile, it is stored with your public profile. It is included in order messages so the delivery person can call you, and buyers can use it to message you on WhatsApp when they pick an item up themselves. It is not displayed in the app. Leave it empty if you do not want to share it.

Items you post. Photos, title, price, description, category and condition of your items are public while the item is on sale. Items you mark as sold are visible only to you.

Food places (optional). If you sell food, the name of your food place, its photo, WhatsApp number, approximate address, opening hours, open/closed status and menu (dishes and prices) are stored. They become public once the developer approves your food place.

Delivery profile (delivery accounts). Delivery persons store their WhatsApp number, vehicle type, areas they cover, short description and availability with their public profile so buyers can contact them.

Basket. Items you add to your basket are saved only on your phone, not on our servers.

Orders. When a signed-in buyer sends an order, the app stores a short record: the item (or the food place and the dishes ordered), the seller or food place, the delivery person (if any), the time, and the buyer's account. The order message itself, including any notes you add to a dish, is sent through WhatsApp, not through our servers.

Ratings and reviews. After an order, a buyer can rate the seller, food place or delivery person with 1 to 5 stars and an optional comment. The rating, comment, date and the buyer's profile name are public on the rated person's profile.

Reports. When you report an item or a seller, we store your account ID, what you reported, the reason and your optional comment. Reports are visible only to the developer, and we never tell anyone who reported them.

Moderation. To keep the app safe, the developer can remove an item, hide a food place, or block an account that breaks the rules (for example scams). A blocked account is signed out and its items are hidden. Only a yes/no "blocked" mark is stored.

Verification badges. To get a verified badge, you meet the developer in person. Your ID card and phone number are checked visually; we never take or keep a copy of your ID. Only the fact that you are verified (yes/no) is stored.

Camera and photos. The app asks for camera and photo access only so you can take or choose photos of your items, your food place and your profile picture.

The app has no ads and no analytics, and does not track your location.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Firebase (Google) — sign-in (Firebase Authentication) and the database (Cloud Firestore). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudinary — stores and delivers item, food place and profile photos. See Cloudinary's Privacy Policy.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram (Meta) — when you tap a WhatsApp or Instagram button, the conversation happens in those apps under Meta's privacy policies, not ours.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Where Your Data Is Stored (Transfers Outside Morocco)

Tanout Shop does not run its own servers. Your data is stored and processed by:

  • Google Firebase — profiles, items, food places, orders, ratings and reports are stored in a Firestore database located in Belgium (European Union). Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google).
  • Cloudinary (United States and other countries) — item, food place and profile photos.

These transfers are carried out in accordance with Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data. Belgium is on the list of countries recognised by the CNDP as providing adequate protection. Photos are sent to Cloudinary in the United States only when you choose to upload them.

How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account, profile and delivery profile: until you delete your account.
  • Items: until you delete them or your account. Items are hidden from buyers 30 days after posting unless renewed.
  • Food place and menu: until you delete your food place or your account.
  • Ratings and reviews you wrote: they stay on the rated person's profile, because they are part of that person's rating. If you delete your account and want your reviews removed too, email us and we will remove them.
  • Photos: photos of deleted items and deleted accounts are removed from Cloudinary within 30 days.
  • Orders: kept up to 12 months (they let buyers rate the people they ordered from and help with fraud checks), then deleted.
  • Reports: kept until reviewed, and at most 12 months.

Your Rights (Law 09-08)

Under Moroccan Law No. 09-08 you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to object to its processing, and to have it deleted. You can correct your profile at any time in the app (My account → Edit profile). For anything else, email rachid.aitbaih95@gmail.com from the Gmail address you signed in with. You may also contact the Moroccan data protection authority, the CNDP (www.cndp.ma).

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account yourself in the app: My account → Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your profile, all your items, your food place and menu, your delivery profile, your basket and your sign-in account. You can also ask us to do it: see how to delete your Tanout Shop account.

Children's Privacy

Tanout Shop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard services (Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules) so that only you can change your profile and items, and only the people involved can see an order. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
