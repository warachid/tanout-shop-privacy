Delete your Tanout Shop account

Tanout Shop, by Rachid (Tanout Dev). Last updated: October 22, 2026

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Tanout Shop and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Go to My account → scroll to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account, confirm, then confirm again with Google.

Option 2: ask us

If you can't use the app, send a message saying "Delete my Tanout Shop account" together with the Gmail address you signed in with:

Email: rachid.aitbaih95@gmail.com
Instagram: @rachid.x_x

We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by replying to you.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name, email, Google profile photo).
  • Your public profile (name, photo, approximate address) and your private phone number.
  • All your items (photos, titles, prices, descriptions).
  • Your food place and its menu (name, photo, WhatsApp number, address, opening hours, dishes).
  • Your delivery profile (WhatsApp number, vehicle, areas, description).
  • Your basket (saved on your phone).
  • Photos are removed from our photo storage (Cloudinary) within 30 days.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Order records you took part in (item, seller, delivery person, time), linked only to account IDs that no longer exist: kept up to 12 months for fraud checks, then deleted.
  • Reports you sent or that concern you: kept until reviewed, at most 12 months.
  • Ratings and reviews you wrote stay on the rated person's profile. Ask us (email above) and we remove them too.

More details in our privacy policy.

Français : supprimer votre compte

  1. Dans l'application : Mon compte → Paramètres → Supprimer mon compte, puis confirmez avec Google.
  2. Ou écrivez-nous (e-mail ou Instagram ci-dessus) avec l'adresse Gmail utilisée pour vous connecter. Suppression sous 7 jours.

Sont supprimés : votre compte, votre profil, vos articles, votre endroit pour manger et son menu, votre profil livreur, et vos photos (sous 30 jours). Les commandes et signalements, liés seulement à des identifiants, sont gardés 12 mois au maximum. Les avis que vous avez écrits restent sur le profil de la personne notée ; écrivez-nous pour les supprimer aussi.

العربية: حذف حسابك

  1. من داخل التطبيق: حسابي ← الإعدادات ← حذف حسابي، ثم أكّد بحساب Google.
  2. أو راسلنا (البريد الإلكتروني أو إنستغرام أعلاه) مع عنوان Gmail الذي سجلت به الدخول. يتم الحذف خلال 7 أيام.

يتم حذف: حسابك، ملفك الشخصي، منتجاتك، محل الأكل وقائمته، ملف عامل التوصيل، وصورك (خلال 30 يوماً). سجلات الطلبات والبلاغات، المرتبطة فقط بأرقام الحسابات، تُحفظ 12 شهراً كحد أقصى. التقييمات التي كتبتها تبقى في ملف الشخص الذي قيّمته؛ راسلنا لحذفها أيضاً.
